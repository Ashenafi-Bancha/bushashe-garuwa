# BUSHASHE GARUWA — PREMIUM CULTURAL HERITAGE & TOURISM WEBSITE

Design and build a complete, production-quality, responsive frontend website for **Bushashe Garuwa**, a unique cultural heritage and tourism destination in Wolaita, Ethiopia.

This is NOT a generic hotel, restaurant, safari, or tourism website.

Bushashe Garuwa is a **living Wolaita heritage destination** combining:

* Wolaita family history and generations
* Historical timeline
* Traditional cultural houses
* Historical trees and plants
* Animals and a small zoo/wildlife experience
* Wolaita cultural artifacts
* Traditional clothing
* Music and dance
* Traditional food and cultural food experiences
* Oral history and storytelling
* Library services
* Digital cultural archive
* Guesthouse
* Restaurant
* Bar
* Cultural events
* Educational/school visits
* Heritage tours
* Visitor experiences
* Future QR-based digital heritage experiences

The website should make visitors feel that they are **entering a living cultural world**, not simply visiting a business.

# 47. FINAL DESIGN FEEL

The final result should feel like:

**A premium digital cultural experience created specifically for Wolaita.**

When someone lands on the homepage, they should immediately understand:

**This is a place with a story.**

When they explore the heritage section:

**This is a place with history.**

When they explore experiences:

**This is a place I can experience.**

When they see the guesthouse and restaurant:

**This is a place I can stay and enjoy.**

When they discover the library and archive:

**This is a place where knowledge is preserved.**

And when they reach the final CTA:

**I want to visit Bushashe Garuwa.**

Prioritize **authenticity, storytelling, photography, cultural respect, usability and visual elegance** over excessive UI decoration.

Create the design as a complete cohesive system with desktop, tablet and mobile responsive layouts.

The final interface should look like a **high-end international cultural heritage destination while remaining authentically Wolaita.**

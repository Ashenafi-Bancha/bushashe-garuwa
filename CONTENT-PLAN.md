# Photos and film needed for the website

This is the shooting list. Everything here has a place waiting for it on the website:
where a photo is missing, the page shows a woven green panel with the name of what belongs there.

**Where to put the files**

- Photos: `frontend/photos-originals/<folder>/` (the folders below), then run `pnpm photos`
- Film: `frontend/media-originals/video/hero-loop.mp4`, then run `pnpm video`
- Send them exactly as the camera made them. No filters, no borders, no watermarks, no text on the photo.

**How each photo should be taken**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Size | At least 2400 pixels on the long side. Straight from the camera, not from WhatsApp or Telegram |
| Shape | Wide (landscape) unless the list says upright |
| Light | Early morning or late afternoon. Avoid harsh midday sun and flash |
| People | Ask permission before photographing guests. For portraits, one clear face, eyes open, no one cut in half at the edge |
| Keep steady | No heavy filters or beauty effects. The website's own colours do the work |

Priority: **1** is needed for the launch, **2** soon after, **3** when there is time.

## The film

**1. The hero film** — the short silent loop behind the home page title.

- 10 to 20 seconds, no speech, no music needed (it plays silently)
- One slow movement: walking through the gate, or a slow pass across the grounds
- Steady. Use a gimbal, a slider, or a drone, not a shaky hand
- Give me the original; `pnpm video` makes the small web copy

**2. The story film** — 60 to 90 seconds, for the launch and for social media.

- The grounds, the houses, food being prepared, people arriving, an elder speaking
- A few words from Commissioner Fiseha Garedew about why the family keeps this place
- Subtitles in English, so officials and visitors both follow it
- Upload it to the Bushaashe Garuwa YouTube channel and send me the link; the website plays it from there so the site stays fast

**3. Short upright clips** — 15 to 30 seconds each, for Telegram, TikTok and Instagram. Filmed upright, not turned sideways afterwards.
